## 摘要
大语言模型（LLM）正越来越多地与知识图谱（KG）相结合，以将推理建立在结构化证据之上。然而，大多数基于LLM的KGQA方法依赖对已有图边的遍历，当推理路径因事实缺失而断裂时便变得不可靠。另一类让LLM生成缺失知识的方法则存在引入幻觉证据的风险。我们提出XoG（eXplore-over-Graph），一个面向不完整KG多跳问答的框架，它从学习到的图结构中而非LLM的参数化知识中恢复缺失的推理路径。XoG将类型级实体-关系统计用于识别候选关系，并与KG嵌入相结合以检索合理的缺失实体，同时将LLM用作语义选择器与推理器。这些机制被整合到一个迭代式的规划-探索-推理过程中。在WebQSP、CWQ以及基于Wikidata的BRINK基准上的实验表明，XoG在完整KG上仍具竞争力，并且在KG不完整的情况下持续优于那些未经过任务特定KGQA训练的同类方法。这些增益在多种LLM主干模型上均得以保持，说明仅靠更强的LLM并不能解决图证据缺失的问题。与一种密切相关的基于规划的方法相比，XoG还将LLM的token消耗最多降低了33%。

## Abstract
Large language models (LLMs) are increasingly combined with knowledge graphs (KGs) to ground reasoning in structured evidence. However, most LLM-based KGQA methods rely on traversing existing graph edges and become unreliable when reasoning paths are broken by missing facts. Alternatives that ask LLMs to generate missing knowledge risk introducing hallucinated evidence. We introduce XoG (eXplore-over-Graph), a framework for multi-hop question answering over incomplete KGs that recovers missing reasoning paths from learned graph structure rather than LLM parametric knowledge. XoG combines type-level entity-relation statistics to identify candidate relations with KG embeddings to retrieve plausible missing entities, using the LLM as a semantic selector and reasoner. These mechanisms are integrated into an iterative planning-exploration-reasoning process. Experiments on WebQSP, CWQ, and the Wikidata-based BRINK benchmark show that XoG remains competitive on complete KGs and consistently outperforms comparable methods without task-specific KGQA training under KG incompleteness. These gains persist across multiple LLM backbones, indicating that stronger LLMs alone do not resolve missing graph evidence. XoG also reduces LLM token consumption by up to 33% compared with a closely related planning-based approach.
